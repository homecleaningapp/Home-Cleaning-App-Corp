This Privacy Policy describes how WeWipe ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application.

WeWipe is developed and operated by Damien De Lombaert, an independent individual developer (the "Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). The Developer acts as the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. The App is provided as a free service and is intended for use as-is.

By downloading, installing, or using WeWipe, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect the following information from your authentication provider:

  • Display name (your full name as provided by the provider)
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL (when provided by the provider)

This information is used to identify you within your household and display your profile to other household members.

If you sign in with Apple, you may choose Apple's "Hide My Email" feature. In that case we receive a private relay email address instead of your real email address, and we use that relay address as your identifier within the App.

1.2 Household & Activity Data

When you use the App, the following data is created and stored:

  • Household information: household name, member list, roles (admin/member), household settings, and invite codes
  • Tasks: task names, descriptions, frequency, point values, schedules, and assigned members
  • Assignments: task assignments, due dates, completion status, and completion history
  • Points & rankings: earned points, point adjustments, and performance statistics
  • Bounties: extra task definitions, completion logs, and reward records
  • Memos: shared notes, checklists, and categories created within your household
  • Vacation periods: start/end dates and related scoring baselines for vacation mode

This data is shared with all members of your household to enable collaborative task management.

1.3 Device & Technical Data

  • Push notification tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens are stored to deliver push notifications to your device
  • Crash reports & diagnostics: we use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash and error data. This may include your device model, operating system version, app version, app state at the time of a crash, and a unique installation identifier generated by Crashlytics. Some diagnostic log entries may also include your email address so we can trace issues affecting a specific account. This data is used solely to diagnose and fix technical problems
  • App preferences: your language preference, theme mode (light/dark), and consent status are stored locally on your device
  • Home screen widget data: if you add the optional home screen widget, a snapshot of your current tasks, points, and household ranking is stored locally on your device so the widget can display it. This snapshot stays on your device

1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or biometric data
  • Advertising identifiers, browsing history, or cookies

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: to verify your identity and allow you to sign in securely
  • Household collaboration: to enable task sharing, assignment tracking, and communication between household members
  • Notifications: to send you task reminders, weekly digests, and household updates
  • App improvement: to identify and fix bugs using crash reports and diagnostic data
  • Gamification: to calculate and display points, rankings, and performance statistics
  • Security & integrity: to protect the App and its users against abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access

3. Legal Basis for Processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Performance of a contract: processing your account information, household data, and notifications is necessary to provide the Service you request
  • Consent: we ask for your consent before collecting data (through the in-app consent screen), and your device asks for your consent before push notifications are enabled. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 7)
  • Legitimate interests: we process crash reports, diagnostic data, and security-related data to keep the App stable, secure, and free of abuse
  • Legal obligation: we may process data where required to comply with applicable law

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services, specifically:

  • Cloud Firestore for all household, task, and user data
  • Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in management
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notification delivery
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase for scheduled tasks such as generating assignments and sending reminder notifications

Firebase infrastructure is hosted on Google Cloud servers and is subject to Google's data protection practices. We rely on Firebase's built-in security features, including encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL) and Firestore security rules that restrict data access to members of the relevant household.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

5.1 Within Your Household

Your name, profile photo, tasks, assignments, points, and memos are visible to all members of your household. This is essential for the collaborative nature of the App. Anyone holding a valid household invite code may join the household and gain access to this shared data (see Section 9).

5.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud (Google) — database, authentication, notifications, crash reporting, and scheduled functions
  • Google Sign-In — authentication
  • Apple Sign-In — authentication on iOS devices

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the Developer.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active and you remain a member of a household. Specifically:

  • Account & profile data: retained until you delete your account
  • Household data: retained as long as the household exists and has active members
  • Crash reports & diagnostics: retained according to Firebase Crashlytics' default retention period (up to 90 days), then automatically removed or aggregated
  • Push notification tokens: updated as your device refreshes them and removed when you sign out or delete your account

7. Your Rights & Controls

You have the following rights regarding your personal data. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, these rights are granted to you under the GDPR:

  • Access: you can view your profile information and all household data directly within the App
  • Rectification: you can update your display name and profile photo through your Google or Apple account, and edit household content within the App
  • Erasure: you can delete your account at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account (see Section 8 for what deletion involves)
  • Restriction & objection: you may ask us to restrict or stop certain processing of your data by contacting us
  • Data portability: you may request a copy of the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used format by contacting us
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time through your device's system settings or the App's settings screen
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw consent for data collection by deleting your account and uninstalling the App; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal
  • Lodge a complaint: you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In France, this is the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13.

8. Account Deletion & Its Effects

When you delete your account from Settings > Account > Delete Account:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently deleted
  • Your roommate profile (name, photo, points, push tokens) is removed from each household you belonged to
  • Content you created that other household members rely on — such as completed task assignments and memos — is anonymized rather than erased: your identity is replaced with "Deleted User" so the household's shared history and statistics remain consistent for the remaining members

Because households are shared spaces, data that belongs to the household as a whole (such as the task list and the household's activity history) is not removed when a single member leaves. If you want all of a household's data removed, every member must delete their account or the household must be emptied.

9. Household Invite Codes

Households are joined using invite codes that are valid for 7 days. Anyone who obtains a valid invite code can join the corresponding household and gain access to its shared data. You are responsible for sharing invite codes only with people you trust.

10. Anonymous Memos

The App includes a feature allowing users to post memos anonymously within their household. When a memo is posted anonymously, the creator's identity is replaced with "Anonymous" and is not displayed to other household members within the App.

11. Children's Privacy

WeWipe is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Some jurisdictions, including certain EU countries, set a higher minimum age for digital consent. If we discover that a child below the applicable age has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

12. Offline Data & International Transfers

The App supports offline functionality. Data may be cached locally on your device to enable use without an internet connection, and is synchronized with our servers when connectivity is restored. Locally cached data is subject to your device's own security measures.

Your data is processed and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may involve transfer to and storage on servers located outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area. Google Cloud relies on recognized safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for such transfers. By using the App, you acknowledge this processing.
