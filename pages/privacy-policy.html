Privacy Policy

Home Cleaning App

Effective date: March 26, 2026

Last updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Home Cleaning ("we", "us", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application. The App is provided as a freemium service and is intended for use as-is.

By downloading, installing, or using Home Cleaning, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect the following information from your account:

  • Full name (display name)
  • Email address
  • Profile photo URL

This information is used to identify you within your household and display your profile to other household members.

1.2 Household & Activity Data

When you use the App, the following data is created and stored:

  • Household information: household name, member list, roles (admin/member), household settings, invite codes
  • Tasks: task names, descriptions, frequency, point values, schedules, and assigned members
  • Assignments: task assignments, due dates, completion status, and completion history
  • Points & Rankings: earned points, point adjustments, performance statistics
  • Bounties: extra task definitions, completion logs, and reward records
  • Memos: shared notes, checklists, and categories created within your household
  • Vacation periods: start/end dates for vacation mode

This data is shared with all members of your household to enable collaborative task management.

1.3 Device & Technical Data

  • Device tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens are stored to deliver push notifications to your device
  • Crash reports: anonymous crash and error data is collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us improve app stability
  • App preferences: your language preference, theme mode (light/dark), and consent status are stored locally on your device

1.4 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or biometric data
  • Browsing history or cookies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: to verify your identity and allow you to sign in securely
  • Household collaboration: to enable task sharing, assignment tracking, and communication between household members
  • Notifications: to send you task reminders, weekly digests, and household updates
  • App improvement: to identify and fix bugs using anonymous crash reports
  • Gamification: to calculate and display points, rankings, and performance statistics

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase services, specifically:

  • Cloud Firestore for all household, task, and user data
  • Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in management
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notification delivery
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting

Firebase infrastructure is hosted on Google Cloud servers and is subject to Google's data protection practices. We rely on Firebase's built-in security features, including encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL) and Firestore security rules to restrict data access.

While we implement reasonable security measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Data Sharing

4.1 Within Your Household

Your name, profile photo, tasks, assignments, points, and memos are visible to all members of your household. This is essential for the collaborative nature of the App.

4.2 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may access your data:

  • Google Play Services — authentication and app distribution
  • Firebase (Google) — database, authentication, notifications, and crash reporting
  • Apple Sign-In — authentication on iOS devices

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active and you remain a member of a household. Specifically:

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Household data: retained as long as the household exists and has active members
  • Crash reports: retained for up to 90 days, then automatically deleted or aggregated
  • Device tokens: updated on each app launch and removed upon account deletion

6. Your Rights & Controls

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: you can view your profile information and all household data directly within the App
  • Correction: you can update your display name and profile photo through your Google or Apple account
  • Deletion: you can delete your account at any time from Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will remove your authentication data and associated profile from the App
  • Notifications: you can disable push notifications at any time through your device's system settings or the App settings
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App

7. Children's Privacy

Home Cleaning is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

8. Anonymous Features

The App includes a feature allowing users to post memos anonymously within their household. When a memo is posted anonymously, the creator's identity is replaced with "Anonymous" and is not disclosed to other household members.

9. Offline Data

The App supports offline functionality. Data may be cached locally on your device to enable use without an internet connection. This cached data is synchronized with our servers when connectivity is restored. Locally cached data is subject to your device's own security measures.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data is processed and stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may involve transfer to servers located outside your country of residence. By using the App, you consent to such transfers. Google Cloud complies with applicable data protection frameworks.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
